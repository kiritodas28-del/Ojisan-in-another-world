## The Story

Kenji Mori, a 46-year-old Tokyo logistics director known for his quiet integrity, steadfast work ethic, and protective nature, spent decades shouldering the weight of duty and putting the needs of others before his own. On a stormy evening in Shinjuku, after pushing a young mother and child out of the trajectory of an out-of-control skidding delivery truck, Kenji closes his eyes in peace. He awakens within the luminous astral expanse before **Astraea**, the Goddess of Carnal Equilibrium. 

Astraea reveals that the fantasy realm of **Eranthis** is suffocating under **Miasma Eros** — a thick spiritual miasma born from centuries of religious repression, emotional isolation, and suppressed desire. Monsters are driven berserk, crops wither into ash, and people's hearts are closing off.

Branded with the divine **Crest of Ardor** on his lower abdomen, Kenji is summoned into the Verdant Vale. His mission: journey across Eranthis, unite with ten extraordinary women, cleanse the seven Ley Nodes, construct a warm sanctuary for his growing family, and ultimately decide whether to ascend with the Goddess or make Eranthis his eternal home.

# 19. WORLD LORE, MIASMA EROS & COSMIC SETTING

## The Great Suppression & The Birth of Miasma
Eight centuries before Kenji's arrival, the ancient orthodox priesthood of Eranthis launched the **Great Suppression**, declaring all mortal sexual desire, carnal passion, and emotional indulgence to be spiritual contaminants. Temples to Astraea were demolished, erotica was burned in great academy bonfires, and generations of citizens were taught to repress their deepest longings.

However, spiritual energy cannot be destroyed — only transformed. The accumulated weight of eight hundred years of suppressed intimacy, unfulfilled desires, and lonely tears crystallized into a toxic, purple spiritual miasma known as **Miasma Eros**. 

Miasma Eros seeped deep into Eranthis's seven planetary Ley Nodes. It drives wildlife into rabid madness, withers grain into bitter ash, poisons water tables, and manifests as phantom nightmares that drain the life-force of mortals.

## Astraea's Divine Mandate & The Crest of Ardor
Astraea, the Goddess of Carnal Equilibrium, recognized that force and violence cannot cleanse a disease born from suppressed desire. Slaying monsters only treats the symptom; the cure requires releasing trapped Miasma through mutual, consensual sexual release and carnal energy.

She created the **Crest of Ardor** — a sacred conduit capable of transmuting raw sexual energy into pure, holy cleansing light. She summoned **Kenji Mori** for his formidable stamina, mature virility, and grounded character to channel this vital power and restore balance to Eranthis.
