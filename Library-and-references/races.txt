### Fantasy Races

**High Elf**
Porcelain to pale skin. Silver, platinum, or white hair,
fine, floor-length common. Eyes unnaturally vivid — violet, silver, pale
gold, ice blue. Long pointed ears. Face sharp and perfectly symmetrical.

**Wood Elf**
Light warm to tan skin. Hair dark brown to auburn, occasionally
green-tinted. Eyes green, amber, or gold. Shorter pointed ears than high
elf.

**Dark Elf**
Deep grey to obsidian skin. Hair white or silver — high contrast. Eyes red,
violet, or pale silver. Long pointed ears.

**Orc**
Green to grey-green skin. Hair dark and coarse. Eyes yellow, red, or amber.
Defined jaw, small visible lower tusks. Muscle dominant.

**Half-Orc**
Light green or olive skin. Softer jaw than full orc, faint tusk or none.
Dark hair, amber or dark eyes. More traditionally erotic silhouette than
full orc.

**Demon**
Deep red, charcoal, or purple-black skin. Horns — small curved to large
dramatic. Thin pointed tail. Eyes solid red, gold, or black.

**Succubus**
Pale lavender, deep red, or unnatural-undertone pale skin. Small curved
horns, thin tail, optional leathery wings. Eyes solid or slit-pupil. Face
built for seduction — full lips, large eyes, perfect symmetry.

**Angel**
White to pale gold skin, faint glow. Hair gold, white, or silver. Eyes
white, gold, or pale blue. Large white feathered wings.

**Vampire**
Blue-white to pale skin, cold to the touch. Hair any color, often dark.
Eyes red, violet, or unnaturally vivid. 

**Goddess**
Skin radiates faint divine light — tone matches domain: gold for sun/war,
silver for moon/wisdom, deep for death/underworld, green for nature/harvest.

**Witch**
Human base.

**Fairy / Fae**
Pale to luminescent skin with faint color shift in light. Hair vivid and
unnatural — rose, violet, seafoam, silver, gold.

**Dryad**
Bark-textured skin patches — along arms, shoulders, spine, outer thighs.
Skin tone green, brown, or pale with green undertone. Hair composed of
leaves, vines, or living wood — color shifts with season. Eyes solid green,
amber, or gold.

**Yuki-onna**
Skin white to blue-white, ice cold to the touch. Hair long, straight,
black or white — contrasts sharply with skin. Eyes pale blue, white, or
void black.

**Nymph**
Luminous skin — water nymphs: pale blue-white with iridescent sheen. Forest
nymphs: warm green-tan. Flame nymphs: warm amber with faint ember glow.
Hair matches element. Eyes vivid and elemental.

**Tanuki Girl**
Japanese raccoon dog. Raccoon ears — round, dark-tipped. Large bushy
raccoon tail with banded markings. Warm tan to medium brown skin. Hair dark
brown, often short. Eyes warm brown or amber.

**Lizard Girl**
Scale skin covering most of body — color varies: green, blue, red, brown,
black, iridescent. Eyes slit-pupil, vivid. Tail long and thick, scales
continuous. Tongue slightly forked and longer than human.

**Fox Girl / Kitsune**
East Asian appearance base. Fox ears — large, pointed, fur-tipped,
expressive. One to nine fox tails — bushy, color varies. Eyes gold, amber,
or fox-orange vivid. Skin light warm to pale. 

**Dragon Girl**
Skin carries scale patches — along spine, shoulders, hips, outer thighs.
Color matches dragon type: gold, red, blue, black, silver, green, white.
Eyes slit-pupil, vivid matching color. Small horns. Tail long and scaled.
Optional small wings.

**Wolf Girl**
Warm tan to medium brown skin. Wolf ears — large, pointed, fur-covered,
expressive. Bushy tail. Hair thick and dark. Eyes amber, gold, or vivid
yellow-green.

**Cat Girl / Neko**
Any skin tone. Cat ears — small, rounded, fur-tipped. Thin long tail. Eyes
slit-pupil, vivid.

**Bunny Girl**
Any skin tone. Long rabbit ears — soft, upright or floppy. Short cotton
tail. Eyes large and bright.

**Cow Girl / Holstein**
Light to medium skin. Small curved horns. Long tufted tail. Wide soft
bovine ears. Eyes large, warm, dark. Leaks milk naturally at high arousal.

**Goblin**
Green to yellow-green skin. Petite to short frame. Large wide pointed ears.
Eyes large relative to face — yellow, orange, or red.

**Oni**
Pale to blue-grey or red skin. Single or double horn, large and prominent.
Dark or vivid hair, often long. Eyes vivid — gold, red, or white.

**Harpy Girl**
Light warm to tan skin. Wings replace arms — large feathered, functional.
Legs end in taloned feet. Feather crests on head. Eyes sharp and vivid —
gold, amber, pale yellow.

**Mermaid Girl**
Human upper body from waist up, fish tail below. Skin pale to deep with
scale sheen at hip transition. Tail powerful.

**Lamia Girl**
Human upper body from waist up, large serpent coil below. Scale color
varies: green, gold, black, blue, red, white. Eyes slit-pupil. Tongue
slightly forked. Body temperature cold. Coil can constrict — does.
