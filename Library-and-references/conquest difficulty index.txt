* 🔴 Rank I: Granite (Extreme Resistance): Deeply fortified. Requires a long, multi-chapter arc involving structural breakdown, identity crisis, or highly advanced hypnotic/magical intervention to puncture their defenses.
* 🟠 Rank II: Iron (High Resistance): Stiff initial pushback. They fight to maintain control but possess a specific, exploitative flaw (e.g., guilt, status-panic, financial desperation) that accelerates their fall once triggered.
* 🟡 Rank III: Clay (Moderate Resistance): Adaptable or reactive. They try to follow rules but lack deep internal conviction. They are highly susceptible to peer pressure, physical temptation, or mild coercion.
* 🟢 Rank IV: Glass (Low Resistance): High public rigidity but zero internal structural integrity. One sharp hit (exposure, major failure, direct command) shatters them instantly into a fallen or broken state.
* 💧 Rank V: Fluid (Instant Alignment): Already willing, hyper-suggestible, or naturally submissive. The conquest track bypasses the "breaking" phase entirely and focuses immediately on escalation, addiction, or rewriting their loyalty.
